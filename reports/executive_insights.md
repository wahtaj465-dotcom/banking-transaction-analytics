# Executive Business Insights

## Revenue Overview

- Total Revenue: ₹398 Million
- Total Transactions: 100,000
- Success Rate: 98%

## Regional Performance

- South region contributed the highest revenue.
- West ranked second in transaction volume.

## Customer Behaviour

- Professionals generated the highest spending.
- Customers aged 25–40 formed the largest customer segment.

## Banking Operations

- UPI was the most frequently used payment method.
- Mobile Banking generated the highest digital transaction volume.

## Branch Performance

- Bengaluru Central was the highest revenue generating branch.

## Operational Insight

- Peak transaction activity occurred during evening hours (8–10 PM).
- Weekend spending was slightly higher than weekday average.
