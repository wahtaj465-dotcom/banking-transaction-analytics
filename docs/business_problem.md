# Banking Transaction Analytics & Anomaly Detection System

## Business Problem

A retail bank wants to analyze customer transaction behaviour across multiple cities and branches. The objective is to identify spending patterns, evaluate merchant performance, monitor transaction success rates, detect anomalous financial transactions, and build an executive dashboard for decision-makers.

## Stakeholders

* CEO → Overall banking KPIs
* Finance Manager → Revenue & spending trends
* Risk Team → Suspicious transactions
* Marketing Team → Customer segmentation
* Regional Managers → Branch performance

## Expected Outcome

Develop an end-to-end analytics solution using Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI that transforms raw banking data into actionable business insights.
